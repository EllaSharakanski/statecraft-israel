Milestone M3 is done. Built from source, the game plays from 15 May 1948 to the end of 1951. It has:

- the Cabinet with appointments;
- the Budget screen;
- the Defence screen with the escalation ladder and reserve call-up;
- sourced historical events from the 1948 war through the 1951 foreign-currency crisis;
- the “Why?” panel;
- autosave and load;
- the browser (WebGL) build.

The browser build was checked locally: both golden runs and a five-turn smoke test, played through the game's own buttons, matched their pinned values in the WebGL player. The itch.io upload moved to the start of M4, so there is no hosted build yet.

Settlements and trees are still placeholder art. Diplomacy, war mode and anything after 1951 are not in this version.

Details: [the M3 release notes](https://github.com/eyalgolan/statecraft-israel/blob/main/docs/m3-release.md).
