---
title: "The GDD becomes the design of record"
date: 2026-10-12
summary: "The owner's game design document, GDD v0.1, now decides what the game becomes. Many of its rules are still open."
---

The owner's game design document, [GDD v0.1](https://github.com/eyalgolan/statecraft-israel/blob/main/docs/GDD.md), is now the design of record. Where an older spec disagrees, the GDD wins.

It plans the MVP as 1948 to 1951 in monthly turns, about 48 of them, with three objectives:

1. Survive the Liberation War.
2. Absorb immigrants: give them housing and employment.
3. Build key infrastructure: the Tel Aviv to Haifa railway and new yishuvim at designated sites.

Completing an objective brings up a card about the real event, with its real date beside the date the player reached it.

Resource amounts, action costs and many rules are still marked open, and none of this is in the playable build yet. The owner approves a phased plan before code changes.

One ruling of the same day: the Deport outcome stays only as a historical possibility that never pays. It carries large Social cohesion and International Support penalties, creates refugees, and is shown soberly through sourced events.
