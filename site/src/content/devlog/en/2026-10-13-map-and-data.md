A selection from the [changelog](https://github.com/eyalgolan/statecraft-israel/blob/main/CHANGELOG.md). All of it is in the repository; a hosted build is still to come.

- **Event cards** have a header photograph: real archival photographs for 24 of the 31 events, from the Meitar Collection at the National Library of Israel (CC BY 4.0), the Nationaal Archief (CC0) and the US National Archives, each with a credit line. No card shows the outcome of the choice it asks for.
- **Railways and main roads of 1948**, traced on the public-domain 1946 Survey of Palestine map. Each route shows from its sourced date: the Burma Road appears on 14 June 1948.
- **Places**: the 271 other places of 1,000 or more people on 15 May 1948, from Village Statistics, 1945, named by their 1948 names, with today's name in a tooltip where a living place carries on.
- **Borders**: the sixteen Mandate sub-districts in force on 15 May 1948, and the 1949 armistice lines from each agreement's date.
- **Units**: the 1948 brigades, 101 Squadron and the first navy ships as tokens at dated, sourced positions. They are drawn on the map; they take no orders.
- **A start screen** with two lines of the Declaration of Independence in the official Hebrew and English. Its painting of the hall is AI-generated, with no identifiable person.
- **Governments**: the Fourth to Seventh Governments, to 1956, with their coalitions and the ministers' dated terms, and the Third Knesset with the 1955 election results.
