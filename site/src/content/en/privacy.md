- This website has **no analytics, no cookies and no tracking**. It stores nothing in your browser.
- Every script, font, image and video on it comes from this site. Nothing loads from another site, and the page's content security policy blocks it if something tried.
- The video on the home and Media pages loads from this site only when you press play. The cinematic trailer is a link to GitHub; nothing from GitHub loads until you follow it.
- The site is static and hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub, as the host, logs and stores each visitor's IP address for security purposes, as [GitHub's documentation on Pages](https://docs.github.com/en/pages/getting-started-with-github-pages/about-github-pages#data-collection) says. The project does not see those logs. [GitHub's privacy statement](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement) applies to them, and to GitHub's own pages when you follow a link there.
