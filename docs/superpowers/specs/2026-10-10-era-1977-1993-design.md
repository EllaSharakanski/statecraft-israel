This document says what the player does from the formation of Begin's government in June 1977 to the eve of the Oslo
signing in September 1993, and how each system of today's sim serves that period, by reuse or by a stated new piece.
It begins where the 1957–1977 chapter ends (20 June 1977) and ends where #52 begins. It writes no code and no content.
The work items are listed in §12 and drafted as issues in "Detailed issues".

Facts quoted here size the design. Each one names its source and the day it was checked. A fact marked *to verify*
has no checked source yet, or its sources disagree. The research issues (§12) settle it before any content uses it.

**Decisions.** Fourteen owner decisions, D1–D14, are listed with their recommended defaults in §13. The rest of the
document assumes the defaults. Three bear on work that is already filed and should be decided before it is built:
D9 (the map box of #129 stops at 33.5 N, short of Beirut; widening it later would renumber every hex a second time),
D10 (money and inflation: the chapters design's display-only currency rule, #66, does not cover a 1984 inflation
near 400% a year) and D3 (this era needs the 1957–1977 design's D3 and D12 adopted as written; it adds no amendment
of its own to a pinned golden).

## 1. The rules this design keeps

1. **The spec's perspective rule** (spec §1; `docs/CONTENT_POLICY.md` §1). Narration and terms are Israeli-perspective,
   from mainstream Israeli historiography, with one sourced value per fact and no opposing-narrative panels. Egypt,
   Syria, Jordan, Iraq, Lebanon and its militias, the PLO, Hezbollah and Hamas, the superpowers and the UN are AI
   actors with their own goals.
2. **What holds regardless** (CONTENT_POLICY §2): every fact is sourced; no quotes are invented; nothing rewards an
   atrocity; the Holocaust is reacted to and never played; real people appear in their public roles; living people
   stay out of a shareable build until a legal review is recorded (spec §5; chapters design §13).
3. **No reset.** The 1957–1977 chapter carries into this one by the chapters design's deviation carry (its D1).
4. **Earlier goldens do not move.** `GoldenRun.Hash1950`, `Hash1952`, `Hash1957` and `SMOKE OK 5 turns` (the Founding
   goldens), and `Hash_sinai_to_upheaval_1967`, `Hash_sinai_to_upheaval_1977`, the 1957–1977 chain golden and
   `carry/sinai_to_upheaval.reference.yaml`, stay the same through every item in §12 (§9.2).
5. **Data decides, code implements** (chapters design §1.4, §5). This era adds **no new `systems` switch, no new
   engine abstraction and no new engine rule.** It reuses the chapters items (#56–#67), the M4 systems (#21 war mode,
   #22 defence depth, #105 actor standing, #111 dated rules, #19 legacy report, #23 map actions) and the 1957–1977
   design's `turn_anchors` (its D12, which this era needs but does not change). Where a new piece could be argued
   for, it is an owner decision with the smallest option recommended: a larger map (D9, data and tools only), money
   in an era with hyperinflation (D10, content and display data only), and a map action for the Sinai withdrawal
   (D6, not built).
6. **The reference run is history.** A policy's `default` is its level at the start of its window. Each later
   historical change is a hard event's `set_policy` on a sourced date, so the `HistoricalAI` reproduces history
   without sending policy orders (the Yishuv design §1.7; the 1957–1977 design §1.6).
7. **The sensitive-content rules extend here** (M4 D8; the 1957–1977 design D7; §10). Massacres and terror against
   Israelis are react-only. Israeli operations are decided at the level of policy, doctrine or operation, never at the
   level of civilian harm. Israeli acts that killed civilians, or that Israeli inquiries found wrong, are on an owner
   include/omit list, and nothing is omitted without the owner.

## 3. The era

### 3.1 The `content/eras.yaml` record (D1)

```yaml
  - id: upheaval_to_oslo            # D1: id and name are owner choices
    name: From the Upheaval to Oslo
    from: 1977-06-20                # the 1957–1977 era's `to` (its D10 settles 20 or 21 June; §9.1)
    to: 1993-09-13                  # the Oslo I signing, which opens #52 (D1)
    player_office: [{ from: 1977-06-20, office: prime_minister }]   # the Yishuv design's key, if adopted (#56)
    systems:
      legislature: state
      map_actions: [housing]        # plus #23's actions when they land; the 1990–91 caravan sites use housing (§5.4)
    start: start/upheaval_to_oslo.yaml
    carry: carry/upheaval_to_oslo.yaml
```

- **No new switch.** `war_mode` is absent, which the 1957–1977 design asks #81 to define as `true`. `economy: state`
  as today.
- **`rules/upheaval_to_oslo.yaml`** holds only what this era changes: its economy values (§5.3) and its
  `turn_anchors` (D13, §3.3). Changes inside the era (the 1980 and 1985 currency changes, the 1983 bank crisis, the
  1985 plan) are dated rule values (#111) or events, not era boundaries.
- **Why one era.** A boundary at the 1984 unity government or at the 1985 plan was considered. The state, Knesset,
  offices and turn grid are the same throughout; the 1985 plan is a policy package inside the effects web (§5.3), not
  a new rule set. One era keeps one calibration, one carry file and one legacy report at a natural end.
- **Why the end is the Oslo signing.** #51 says "to the eve of Oslo", and #52 opens "from the Oslo accords". The
  boundary on 13 September 1993 puts the Madrid talks, the 1992 change of government and the secret channel (*to
  verify* its dates) in this chapter, and the signing, the Knesset vote and their consequences in #52. The era's final
  part (Rabin's government) is then about 14 months long.

### 5.3 Economy, inflation and money (D10)

### 10.3 Violence against civilians: rules (D7, extending the 1957–1977 D7 and M4 D8)

1. **Operations are decided at the level of policy, doctrine or operation.** Litani, Opera, the 1982 war, the
   security zone, Wooden Leg and Accountability are orders at the level the government gave them, with the sourced
   aim. Civilian deaths are never an option and never rewarded. Where civilians were killed, the event carries
   `war-casualties` and states the sourced fact.
2. **Attacks on Israelis are react-only.** The events in §10.2's `terror` row are events the government responds to;
   the options are its real responses and their alternatives. No option rewards the attacker's act or an
   indiscriminate response.
3. **Attacks by Israelis on Arabs are react-only too.** The Jewish Underground and the Rishon LeZion killings
   (*to verify*) are events the government responds to (the Shin Bet's arrests, the trials, the commutations), with
   no option that rewards the act. People who are not office holders are named by role, not by name (§10.6).
4. **Israeli acts that killed civilians or were found wrong are on an owner list,** each with include, omit or
   how-to-show. Research item 6 drafts it with dates and one sourced fact each. Candidates:

   - the Bus 300 affair (1984–86): the killings in custody, the cover-up and the pardons;
   - Wooden Leg's deaths (1 Oct 1985, *to verify* the toll);

   - the intifada's measures: the response doctrine of early 1988 (*to verify* the wording in an Israeli source; no
     quote), administrative detention, house demolitions, school closures and deportations (from 1985 and the 415 of
     December 1992);
   - Temple Mount, 8 Oct 1990 (17 killed; the Israeli commission's findings);
   - the Khiam detention centre run by the SLA (§2.3);

### 10.4 Sabra and Shatila

This is the era's most sensitive event and is fixed by design, subject only to the owner's review of its text:

- **React-only, Israeli-perspective.** The killings were carried out by the Lebanese Forces militia (§2.4). The
  player never orders, permits or prevents them by an option, and no war-mode order touches the camps (M4 D4(e)
  extended). The event fires on its sourced date as a hard event.

  dates, members and findings (indirect responsibility; Sharon's personal responsibility and the recommendation that
  he leave the Defence Ministry; Eitan; others named in the report, *to verify* from the report's text) in the
  commission's sourced wording, never as an invented quote.
- **The options are the government's real responses:** the commission's establishment (28 Sep 1982), and later the
  report's implementation (Sharon's departure from the ministry on 14 Feb 1983). The protest of 25 Sep 1982 and
  Grunzweig's killing are linked events.
- **Nothing rewards it.** No option of the event or its chain raises treasury, support or a legacy score by
  minimising or concealing; options that delay or refuse an inquiry cost standing and support by sourced direction.
- **The death toll** is one value from a mainstream Israeli source (the commission's report is the candidate, *to
  verify*), not the article's range; D11.
