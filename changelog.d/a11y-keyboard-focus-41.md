### Fixed

- Keyboard: the Cabinet's seat and candidate lists no longer put their scroll bars in the Tab order, so Tab goes from
  the last row straight to the next control instead of stopping on a bar that cannot be used from the keyboard.
- Keyboard: a city panel button that the press itself leaves gone or disabled no longer drops the focus. The Assign
  stepper's minus disables itself at zero; the focus now goes to the next control that can take it in the same part,
  rather than nowhere, so the following Tab carries on instead of starting again at the first screen.
