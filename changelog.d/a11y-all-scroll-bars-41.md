### Fixed

- Keyboard: no scroll bar takes a Tab stop anywhere now, not only in the Cabinet. A scroll view hides its bar while
  its content fits, so the stray stop came back as soon as a list overflowed: in a short window the Budget's bar took
  one Tab stop and the Defence body's took two in a single lap, and the lists that grow during a game (the Why list,
  the Timeline, the turn report, the end screen) would have done the same at any size. Dragging the bar and the mouse
  wheel are unchanged, and PageUp, PageDown, Home and End still scroll the open screen from the keyboard.
